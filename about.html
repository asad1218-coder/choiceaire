35+ Years of Craftsmanship Over Corporate Sales

While massive private equity rollups buy out local contractors to push commission-driven replacements, Choice Aire has remained independently owned, engineering-focused, and family-operated in Akron since 1989.

The Choice Aire Heritage

Built on Technical Truth & Factory Precision

In 1989, Choice Aire was founded with a single service truck and a non-negotiable rule: Never sell a homeowner equipment they don't need, and never leave an installation until airflow is calibrated within 1% of factory specifications.

Over the past 35 years, our Akron facility at 1940 Englewood Ave. has expanded to include a full custom sheet metal fabrication workshop, an in-house apprentice training lab, and a 24/7 dedicated dispatch fleet serving Summit, Stark, Portage, Medina, Cuyahoga, and Wayne counties.

Today, we have completed over 14,000 installations and maintenance calls while maintaining an unblemished A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.
