The Henderson Residence: Eliminating the 10-SEER Freon Trap

The Hendersons had lived with a 19-year-old builder-grade single-stage AC and 80% gas furnace. In summer 2024, their monthly electric bills peaked at $460, while their second floor master bedroom remained 7°F hotter than the rest of the house.

Engineering Solution: Choice Aire conducted a Manual J heat load calculation, removed the obsolete R-22 system, fabricated custom aerodynamic sheet metal transitions, and installed a Bryant Evolution Extreme 24 SEER2 Variable Inverter Heat Pump with smart zone sensors.

"Our monthly financing payment is $129/month, but our electric bill dropped by $285/month. Choice Aire literally put $156 back into our pockets every month from day one." — Mark & Karen Henderson, Canton, OH

The Bistro at Falls: Friday Night Restaurant Rescue

On a 92°F humid Friday afternoon just before dinner service, the main dining room rooftop packaged unit and walk-in prep chiller tripped out. The internal temperature of their walk-in began rising toward the critical 41°F health code threshold, putting $14,500 of fresh seafood and prime beef at immediate risk.

Emergency Dispatch: Choice Aire's Akron commercial rapid-response van arrived in 28 minutes. The technician diagnosed a dead dual run capacitor and burned relay, replaced both with heavy-duty OEM parts from the truck, and restored full cooling before the 5:30 PM dining rush opened.

"Any other company told us Monday morning. Choice Aire had their tech on our roof in under half an hour. They literally saved our entire weekend business." — Chef Julian Rossi, General Manager

St. Luke Medical Arts: Surgical Silence & Germicidal Purity

A multi-suite medical and dental facility in West Akron was failing indoor acoustic compliance due to rattling sheet metal vibrations directly above patient examination rooms. Additionally, post-pandemic healthcare ventilation guidelines required sterile particulate filtration.

Engineering Solution: Choice Aire engineered neoprene spring-isolated curb mounts to eliminate mechanical vibration transfer, added internal acoustic duct liner, and installed in-duct Bryant Perfect Air MERV-16 filtration with continuous UV-C germicidal emitters.

"Our physicians can now perform delicate procedures in total silence, and our quarterly air quality audits scored in the top 1% of Ohio outpatient clinics." — Dr. Eleanor Vance, Facility Director
